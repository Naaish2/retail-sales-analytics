# Business Insight Report

## Executive Summary

The Walmart sales analytics project evaluates sales, profit, product performance, regional trends, customer segments, and discount effectiveness. The analysis supports inventory, marketing, and pricing decisions.

## Key Metrics

- Total sales and total profit are calculated in `outputs/kpi_summary.csv`.
- Monthly revenue and profit trends are available in `outputs/monthly_sales_trend.csv`.
- Store, product, region, category, and customer segment summaries are available in `outputs/`.

## Core Insights

1. Product performance should be measured by both revenue and profit. High-revenue products are not always the most profitable if discounting is heavy.
2. Region-wise analysis helps identify where sales strategy is working and where leadership should review inventory, staffing, or pricing.
3. Customer segment analysis supports targeted campaigns for high-value groups.
4. Discount analysis helps detect products or categories where promotions increase sales but reduce margin.
5. Monthly trends reveal seasonal peaks that should guide replenishment and promotional planning.

## Recommendations

1. Reduce discounts on products with weak profit margins.
2. Increase inventory for top-selling products before peak months and holiday weeks.
3. Focus marketing spend on customer segments with strong revenue and profit contribution.
4. Review low-performing regions for pricing, assortment, and local demand issues.
5. Track monthly revenue and margin together, not sales alone.
